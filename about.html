ABOUT EGYPT 360

Discover Egypt.
Experience the Story.

Egypt 360 is a frontend tourism website designed to make discovering Egypt easier, more exciting, and more engaging.

The project brings together Egypt's ancient history, rich culture, breathtaking destinations, and unforgettable experiences in one connected digital journey.

Through interactive destination exploration, cultural content, visual galleries, and trip planning, Egypt 360 helps users discover both famous landmarks and hidden destinations across Egypt.

Our goal is to combine storytelling, visual design, and user-centered navigation to create an enjoyable digital tourism experience that inspires travelers to explore Egypt and plan their next adventure.
